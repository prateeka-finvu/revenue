How revenue is computed — "Active Users"/"Unique Users" (same billing model) use the AU count as-is for the current month; "Data Fetch"/"Fix Billing" treat the DF count as a month-to-date total and project it to a full month using the as-of date (day-of-month ÷ days in that month). Every subsequent month in the FY grows that baseline usage figure by (1 + CMGR) compounded, with yield held constant. Revenue = usage × yield for every month. FIUs with a billing model that isn't recognized, or missing from a config, are shown separately rather than guessed.
